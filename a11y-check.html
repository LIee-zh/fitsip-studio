Who we are

About the studio

Fit & Sip pairs small-group training with a healthy café in one Chengdu space. We train hard, sip clean, and keep the coaching personal.

Our story

We opened as a neighbourhood studio for people who want strength, cycle and mobility classes without a crowded gym. The café grew out of the same idea: simple smoothies, bowls and drinks made with whole ingredients, no added sugar.

Coaches keep every session personal, plan around the classes you book, and notice when members stop coming. The studio and the café are meant to fit each other, not be two separate stops.

Whether you come for strength, cycle, mobility or a post-workout drink, the goal is the same: progress you can keep, in a space that feels like yours.

Head coach

Strength & conditioning

Leads foundations and high-interval sessions; builds plans for members new to training.

Cycle coach

Indoor cycling

Runs morning cycle classes focused on cardio, rhythm and steady effort.

Mobility coach

Stretch & recovery

Guides mobility classes that help members recover between harder sessions.

What we value

  • Personal coaching in small classes, not large drop-in crowds.
  • Honest whole-ingredient food and drinks, with clear ingredients on the menu.
  • A friendly studio where members are recognised between visits.
  • Sustainable habits that last past the first few weeks.
